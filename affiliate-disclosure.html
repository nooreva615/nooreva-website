Transparency matters

Affiliate disclosure

How Nooreva may earn revenue while sharing recommendations.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a special link to a retailer or service. If a reader clicks that link and completes a qualifying action, Nooreva may receive a commission at no additional cost to the reader, depending on the programme's terms.

How we plan to use affiliate links

Nooreva may join affiliate programmes in the future. If we use affiliate links, we will disclose the relationship clearly near the relevant recommendation or link, as well as on this page.

Our editorial approach

Commercial relationships should not replace useful, honest information. We aim to explain why a product may suit a reader, note relevant limitations, and avoid claiming first-hand testing unless it genuinely took place. Readers should check current pricing, availability, specifications, shipping, return terms, and retailer policies before buying.

Questions

Questions about a recommendation or a commercial relationship? Contact nooreva615@gmail.com.
