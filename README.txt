NOOREVA PROFESSIONAL WEBSITE DEMO

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Download and extract the ZIP.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html.
4. Keep styles.css and script.js in the same folder as the HTML pages.

PAGES INCLUDED
- index.html (home)
- blog.html (starter journal topics, search and category filter)
- categories.html
- about.html
- contact.html (form prepares an email via mailto; no server submission)
- privacy.html (template)
- terms.html (template)
- affiliate-disclosure.html (template)
- styles.css (responsive styling)
- script.js (menu, search panel, blog filtering, email form helper)

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
This is a front-end static website demo, not yet a production-ready publishing platform.
- Sample article topics are placeholders, not finished research articles.
- There is no CMS/admin dashboard, database, login, hosted form handler, newsletter signup backend, analytics, affiliate tracking, or live product feed.
- Product cards/categories are editorial starter content; no purchases are processed.
- Privacy, terms, and affiliate pages are templates and should be reviewed and customised before launch.
- Google Fonts uses an internet connection; fallback fonts are included.
- Replace the placeholder editorial content with original useful articles and real imagery you have rights to use.
- Add real affiliate links only after joining the relevant programmes and follow their rules.
- Before launch, test on mobile and desktop, connect a domain/hosting, set up HTTPS, favicon, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, and Search Console.

RECOMMENDED FIRST CONTENT
Publish 5-10 useful original articles in one or two categories first, rather than many thin pages. Each article should answer a specific reader question, explain how you researched recommendations, disclose affiliate relationships near links, and be kept up to date.
